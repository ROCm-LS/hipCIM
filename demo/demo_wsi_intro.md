<p style="font-size: 14px; color: #666; font-style: italic;">
A histopathological whole slide image (WSI) is a digital representation 
of a microscope slide containing a tissue sample. Pathologists use WSIs 
to examine tissues for disease, such as cancer. 
By improving accessibility, enabling accurate diagnoses, and fueling 
research, histopathological WSIs are revolutionizing pathology and 
computational imaging.
</p>
<p style="font-size: 14px; color: #666; font-style: italic;">
For this demonstration, we are using a histopathological whole slide image 
of a lymphoid organ tissue section (such as a lymph node or tonsil). The 
sample is stained with Hematoxylin and Eosin (H&amp;E) &mdash; a standard 
staining method in pathology that makes cell nuclei appear purple/blue 
and highlights cytoplasmic and extracellular components in pink for 
enhanced tissue differentiation. Additionally, the image is stained with 
DAB (3,3'-diaminobenzidine), producing a brown coloration where the 
specific target of interest is present, enabling precise detection of 
biomolecular markers.
</p>
